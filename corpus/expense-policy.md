# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Section 1: General Rules
Expenses must be necessary, reasonable, and wholly related to business.

- Manager approval: Up to Rs. 25,000.
- Above Rs. 25,000: Department head approval is required.

## Section 2: Submission and Reimbursement
- Submit expenses through the HR portal within 30 days.
- Submissions between 30 and 60 days require a written explanation and department head approval.
- Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed.
- Receipts are required for every line item above Rs. 500.
- Approved claims are paid in the next monthly payroll if approved by the 20th.

## Section 3: Local Conveyance
Travel between office, client locations, or other business locations is reimbursable.

- Home-to-base commute is never reimbursable.
- Cab expenses: Actual cost with receipt.
- Personal two-wheeler: Rs. 6 per km.
- Personal car: Rs. 14 per km.
- Mileage log must include date, origin, destination, and distance.

## Section 4: Meals and Entertainment
Client or external partner meals are reimbursable with business purpose and attendee names.

- Maximum: Rs. 1,500 per person.
- Colleague-only meals are not reimbursable except pre-approved team events.
- Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Section 5: Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following are never reimbursed:
- Traffic fines and parking penalties.
- Personal grooming.
- Commuting fuel.
- Late fees caused by delayed submission.
- Claims without a recorded business purpose.
