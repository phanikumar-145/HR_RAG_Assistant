# Leave Policy
## Section 1: Applicability
This policy applies to confirmed employees on the direct payroll of Northwind Technologies Private Limited. Contractors and interns are not covered. The leave year runs from January 1 to December 31.

## Section 2: Casual Leave
Employees receive 12 days of casual leave per year, credited at one day per completed month. Casual leave can be used for personal exigencies, family obligations, and religious observances.

- Minimum leave: Half day.
- Maximum continuous leave: 3 consecutive days.
- Unused casual leave lapses on December 31.
- Casual leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## Section 3: Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for 3 or more consecutive days of sick leave. Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## Section 4: Earned Leave
Earned leave accrues at 18 days per year, or 1.5 days per completed month.

- Employees must provide 10 working days' notice and obtain manager approval.
- Maximum accumulation: 45 days.
- Up to 15 days can be carried forward.
- Excess above 15 days is encashed at basic salary with January payroll.

## Section 5: Bereavement Leave
Employees receive 5 working days of paid leave for the death of an immediate family member. No documentation is required.

## Section 6: Loss of Pay
Absence without available leave balance or approval is treated as loss of pay. Deduction is calculated pro-rata using a 30-day month.
