# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Section 1: Work Arrangements
- On-site employees: 5 days per week.
- Hybrid employees: 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, and one team-selected fixed quarterly day).
- Remote work: Up to 4 days per month in-office by request.

## Section 2: Core Working Hours
Core working hours are 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST on working days.

## Section 3: Remote Work Eligibility
Employees must meet all the following conditions:
- 12 months of continuous service.
- Probation confirmation.
- Performance rating of Meets Expectations or above.
- Manager endorsement.
- Department head approval.

Remote work designation is reviewed every 6 months and may be withdrawn with 30 days' notice.

## Section 4: Technical Requirements
- Broadband speed: Minimum 25 Mbps download and 5 Mbps upload.
- Employees must have a confidential workspace.
- Company equipment must be used, except for approved mobile email.

## Section 5: Working Outside India
Working outside India requires department head and Legal/Tax approval at least 21 days in advance.

Maximum duration: 30 days in a rolling 12-month period.

## Section 6: Attendance
Employees must use badge entry. Hybrid employees who fall below required attendance for two consecutive months will be reviewed and may be changed to On-site status.
